01

Our story

Richville Federated Group of Companies traces its origins to February 1989, with a single detached house in Ayala Alabang Village. With only a handful of basic equipment, a few workers, and minimal support facilities, its owners and colleagues worked as a team, each contributing their own experience and expertise.

Their persistence paid off as the corporation grew in the business of developing and constructing on its acquired properties. Given the room to explore new ideas, the company expanded from acquisition and construction into property management operations — and, later, hospitality.

How accreditation runs

Four steps, with a written decision at the end of the review so you know where you stand.

Step 01

Send your company profile

Email the documents listed above to our procurement desk. Incomplete sets are held, not rejected.

Step 02

Accreditation review

Procurement verifies documents, capacity, and safety record, then confirms your supply category.

Step 03

Project briefing

Accredited suppliers are invited to site briefings and asked to quote against a written scope.

Step 04

Purchase order

Awarded work runs on a written PO with agreed milestones, retention, and payment terms.

Data privacy

How RFGC collects, uses, and protects your personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Last updated: [add date]  ·  Registered with the National Privacy Commission  ·  DPO/DPS Registration No. [add number]

Who is responsible for your data

Richville Federated Group of Companies (RFGC) is the personal information controller for the data collected through this website and across our divisions — The One Residences, Vivere Hospitality Group, Richville Offices, and ReachOutVille Foundation Inc.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us — name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Inquiry details — the property, unit type, or role you are asking about, and the content of your message.
  • Application documents, if you apply for a role or submit a resume.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used only to keep the site working.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your inquiry and route it to the right leasing, hospitality, or corporate team.
  • To process leasing applications, reservations, and employment applications.
  • To send updates about a property you asked about — only where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To meet legal, tax, and regulatory requirements.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the RFGC division handling your request, with service providers bound by confidentiality agreements, and with government agencies where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept for [add period] from your last contact with us. Tenancy, guest, and employment records are kept for the period required by law, then securely disposed of.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, to erase or block, to damages, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, write to our Data Protection Officer using the details below. We respond within the period set by the National Privacy Commission.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies needed to keep pages working and to measure basic traffic. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to the site.
